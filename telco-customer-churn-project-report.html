1. Executive Summary

This project delivers an end-to-end customer churn analytics solution for a telecommunications provider. Using the IBM Telco Customer Churn dataset (7,043 customers), the project covers data ingestion and cleaning, KPI calculation, interactive dashboard design, machine learning–based churn prediction, and actionable business recommendations.

The overall churn rate stands at 26.54% — significantly above the industry average of ~20%. The analysis identifies month-to-month contracts, fiber optic internet service, electronic check payment, and short tenure as the four strongest churn predictors. A Gradient Boosting model achieves a ROC-AUC of 0.840 and flags 1,080 high-risk customers representing $85,810 in monthly revenue at risk.

2. Project Objectives

The project was scoped around two core questions:

  1. What are the key factors causing customers to cancel their service?
  2. Which current customers are at the highest risk of leaving next month?

Five technical deliverables were defined to answer these questions:

  1. Load and inspect the raw CSV dataset
  2. Clean and validate data quality (missing values, inconsistencies, type errors)
  3. Define and calculate Churn Rate, ARPU, and CLV
  4. Build an interactive dashboard with monthly trends filterable by region and product tier
  5. Train a churn prediction model and surface business recommendations

9. Key Findings

🔴
Month-to-month contracts are the #1 churn driver. Customers on flexible monthly plans churn at 42.71% — 15× the rate of two-year customers (2.83%). Contract type has the highest feature correlation with churn (r = 0.405) and is the most actionable lever for retention.
🔴
Fiber optic internet users churn at 41.89% — 2.2× the DSL rate. Despite paying the highest ARPU ($91.50/mo), fiber optic customers are the highest churn segment. This suggests a gap between price and perceived value, likely driven by service quality issues or competitive alternatives.
🔴
Electronic check users churn at 45.29% — the highest of any payment method and nearly 3× the rate of auto-pay customers. Manual payment correlates with disengaged customers who have not automated their relationship with the service.
🟡
New customers (0–12 months) churn at 47.68%. Almost half of all customers acquired in the first year leave before month 13. The first 90 days represent a critical window for onboarding, value demonstration, and relationship-building.
🟡
Absence of TechSupport and OnlineSecurity strongly predicts churn (r = 0.34 and 0.34 respectively). Customers who feel unprotected or unsupported are far more likely to switch. These add-ons are not just revenue — they are retention anchors.
🟡
Premium tier customers churn at 37.81% — the highest of all product tiers. These are the highest-ARPU customers ($86.93/mo), making their churn the most revenue-destructive. Retaining even 20% of churning premium customers would recover ~$8,700/month.
🟢
Long-tenure customers (49–72 months) churn at only 9.51% and carry the highest ARPU ($73.95/mo) and CLV. These customers represent the core loyal base and should be actively rewarded to sustain their relationship and generate referrals.

10. Business Recommendations

1. Contract Upgrade Incentive Programme 🎯 Immediate · High Impact
Offer month-to-month customers a 15–20% discount to switch to annual contracts. Use the ML churn score to prioritise outreach on the 1,080 high-risk customers first. Converting 30% would reduce overall churn rate by an estimated 8–12% and recover approximately $500K annually.
2. 90-Day Onboarding Success Programme 🎯 Short-term · High Impact
Deploy automated touchpoints at Day 7, 30, and 90 for all new customers — tutorials, health checks, and satisfaction micro-surveys. Early intervention in the highest-churn window (0–12 months, 47.7% rate) can reduce first-year churn by an estimated 5–8%.
3. Bundle TechSupport & OnlineSecurity Free for 6 Months 🎯 Short-term · Medium Impact
Gifting these add-ons to customers who don't have them increases stickiness, raises perceived security and value, and creates an upsell event at month 7. Cost is marginal; estimated 3–5% churn reduction across affected segment.
4. Fiber Optic Service Quality Audit 📋 Medium-term · High Impact
Conduct NPS surveys and speed-test audits specifically targeting fiber optic subscribers with tenure <24 months. Identify whether the 41.89% churn rate is driven by service reliability, price shock, or competitive pressure, then act accordingly with SLA improvements, pricing adjustments, or speed upgrades.
5. AutoPay Incentive ($5/mo Discount) 🎯 Immediate · Medium Impact
Electronic check payers churn at 45.29% — 3× the auto-pay rate. A small monthly discount for switching to bank transfer or credit card auto-pay reduces churn risk, lowers payment failure rates, and costs less than the revenue recovered from even a 5% churn reduction.
6. Deploy Churn Score API into CRM 📋 Medium-term · Strategic
Operationalise the Gradient Boosting model (AUC 0.840) as a nightly batch scoring job writing churn probabilities into every CRM customer record. Retention agents will have data-driven context on every customer interaction, enabling proactive saves rather than reactive cancellation handling.
7. Loyalty Rewards for 2+ Year Customers 📋 Long-term · Strategic
Long-tenure customers churn at only 9.51% and generate the highest CLV. Recognise them with exclusive benefits — priority support queues, free service upgrades, referral bonuses. Happy loyal customers organically acquire new customers who are more likely to stay long-term.
